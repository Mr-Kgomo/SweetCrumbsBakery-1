About Sweet Crumbs

Welcome to Sweet Crumbs Bakery, where we specialize in delicious and freshly baked treats with passion.

Sweet Crumbs Bakery is a proudly South African bakery based in Pretoria. We specialize in custom cakes, cupcakes, fresh bread, biscuits, and traditional magwinya.

Every item is made fresh daily in our kitchen.

Sweet Crumbs started in 2023 in a small kitchen in Pretoria, Gauteng. It all began when our founder, Sesi Thembi baked cupcakes for her 8 years old grandchild's birthday. Eveyone felt the love baked within the cupcakes and the whole neighborhood wanted some.

After a birthday celebration of cupcakes, Sesi Thembi's artwork became famous in the community which turned into fresh bread, biscuits, warm magwinya and birthday cakes for the whole community. We believe every bite should feel the love and taste like home.

Our mission is simple: Bake fresh, affordable and delicious treats daily with love. Deliver quality baked treats with excellent customer service..

Visit us today and taste why we are Pretoria's 5 star rated bakery.
